## Description (English)

PlantDoctor - AI-powered plant disease detection app developed by Thai Nguyen University of Agriculture and Forestry (TUAF).

KEY FEATURES:
🔍 Disease Diagnosis: Take a photo of a leaf → AI analyzes → Get results in seconds
💊 Treatment Recommendations: Appropriate treatment methods and pesticide suggestions
🧪 Pesticide Database: 4700+ products approved for use in Vietnam
📚 Disease Library: Detailed information on common diseases in rice, corn, tomato, potato
⚠️ Banned Substance Alerts: Automatic warnings for prohibited pesticides

SUPPORTED CROPS:
Rice, Corn, Tomato, Potato, Grape, Orange, Peach, Pepper, Strawberry, Apple and more.

HIGHLIGHTS:
• Advanced AI with high accuracy on real-world photos
• Results in Vietnamese, easy to understand for farmers
• Directly linked to official Vietnam pesticide catalog (Circular 75/2025)
• Free to use

Developed by Trieu Xuan Hoa - Thai Nguyen University of Agriculture and Forestry.
